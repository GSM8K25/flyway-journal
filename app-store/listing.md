**Description (4000 max):**
Flyway Journal is a hunting logbook built for the blind: big buttons, a dark theme for pre-dawn, and everything saved as you go.

START THE HUNT, THEN JUST TAP +
Tap Start and Flyway saves the time, your GPS spot and the weather: temperature, wind, pressure trend, sky, sunrise and shooting light. Pick the species you expect, then tap + as birds come to hand. Count shots too if you want your birds-per-shot.

EVERYTHING WORTH REMEMBERING
• Species tallies for 50+ North American ducks, geese, cranes, rails and more
• Dog retrieves: give the dog credit
• Bands: number, species, type, photos, and a link to report at reportband.gov
• Notable birds: mounts, full plumage, oddballs and first-of-species, with photos
• Marked locations: blind, decoy spread, where birds were working, a bird down
• Saved spots with notes on access, best wind and parking, plus directions

SEE WHAT'S WORKING
The Season tab shows totals, birds per hunt, your best spots, best wind direction, results by setup and barometer trend, plus a life list of every species you've taken.

YOUR JOURNAL, YOUR PHONE
No account and no ads. Your hunts, spots and photos stay on your iPhone, with an automatic backup in the Files app. Save a full backup or a season spreadsheet (CSV, opens in Excel) whenever you like.

Works with no signal once you're set up. Weather needs a connection, and map areas you've already viewed keep working offline.

Shooting hours, bag limits and open species vary by state and zone. Always check your regulations.

## App Privacy (the "nutrition label")
Choose **"No, we do not collect data from this app."** Everything stays on the device. Weather and map requests go to Open-Meteo and Esri in real time and nothing is stored or linked to the user, which Apple's definition doesn't count as collection.

## App Review notes (paste into "Notes" under App Review Information)
Flyway Journal is a personal waterfowl hunting logbook. There's no login or account, and all data is stored on the device.

To try it: tap "Start today's hunt" (allow location; it's used to tag the hunt and fetch local weather), tap "+ Pick species for today", check a few species, then tap + on a species to count birds. "Mark here" saves a GPS location, "+ Band" records a band, and the Season tab shows statistics. Season › Save backup writes a file to the app's folder in Files and opens the share sheet.

The app does not sell or facilitate the sale of firearms or ammunition. It is a record-keeping tool, and it reminds users to follow their state's regulations.
