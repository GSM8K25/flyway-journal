Flyway Journal

Privacy policy · Effective October 22, 2026
The short version: your journal lives on your phone. Flyway has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never see your hunts, spots or photos.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter: hunts, bird counts, dog retrieves, band numbers, notes, saved spots, marked locations and photos. It is stored only on your device. In the iPhone app, a copy is also kept in the app's own folder on your phone (visible in the Files app) so your journal survives if the app's storage is cleared. That folder is included in your regular iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on; those backups are handled by Apple under your Apple account, not by us.

Location

With your permission, Flyway uses your phone's location while the app is open to tag a hunt, save spots and mark locations on the map. It never tracks you in the background. Your coordinates are saved only in your journal on your phone.

Services the app contacts

  • Weather — Open-Meteo. To show weather for a hunt, the app sends the hunt's approximate coordinates and date to Open-Meteo. Nothing that identifies you is sent with them.
  • Maps — Esri (and OpenStreetMap on the website version). To draw the map, your phone downloads map and satellite tiles for the area you're viewing, like any map app does.

Like any internet request, these services receive your device's IP address as part of the connection. Their own privacy policies apply to that. Flyway does not send them your name, journal or photos.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it, for example a hunt summary, a backup file or a season spreadsheet sent through the share sheet. Where it goes is up to you.

Deleting your data

Delete a hunt, spot or photo in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its data from your phone, including the app's backup folder. Copies you shared or saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Children

Flyway is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
