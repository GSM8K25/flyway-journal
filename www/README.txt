FLYWAY JOURNAL — free waterfowl hunting logbook (iPhone & Android)

PUT IT ONLINE (free, about 5 minutes, on a computer)
 1. Unzip this file. You'll get a folder called "Flyway-Journal".
 2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and drag the "Flyway-Journal" folder onto the page.
 3. Sign up for the free account it offers so the site stays up.
 4. Optional: Site settings > Change site name, e.g. flyway-journal.netlify.app

INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE
 iPhone: open the address in Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen.
 Android: open it in Chrome > menu > Install app.
 Allow Location when asked (for spots, weather and marked locations).

SHARE IT
 Tap the share button (top right) in the app: QR code, Send link, Copy link.
 Everyone's journal stays private on their own phone.

GOOD TO KNOW
 - Back up now and then: Season tab > Save backup. Season spreadsheet (CSV) opens in Excel.
 - Weather comes from Open-Meteo (free). Maps from OpenStreetMap and Esri imagery.
 - Report bands at reportband.gov.
 - Shooting hours, limits and open species vary by state — always check your regulations.
