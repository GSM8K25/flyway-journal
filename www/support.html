Common questions

Where is my data? On your phone only. There's no account and no cloud sync. In the iPhone app, a copy is kept automatically in Files › On My iPhone › Flyway › Flyway Autobackup.

Moving to a new phone? Restoring your iPhone backup to the new phone brings your journal along. You can also use Season › Save backup, send the file to yourself, then Season › Restore backup on the new phone.

Spots or weather not working? Turn on location in Settings › Flyway › Location (“While Using the App”). Weather needs a signal; you can add it later from the hunt's Details.

Map is blank? Map tiles need a signal the first time you view an area. Areas you've already looked at keep working with no signal.

Banded bird? Report it at reportband.gov.

Shooting hours, bag limits and open species vary by state and zone. Always check your regulations.
